01 // SCIENTIFIC STANDARDS

The Three Methodological Pillars

Rigorous scientific protocols governing human cognitive audits, visual scaling integrity, and machine contextual risk.

PILLAR 01

Cognitive Ergonomics & 4-Chunk Constraints

All user interface evaluations and financial document architectures are audited against biological working memory limits (Cowan, 2001; Miller, 1956). We measure how visual noise and layout complexity induce involuntary heuristic shortcuts.

PILLAR 02

Isometric Geometric Scaling

We establish mathematical protocols for financial data visualization that prevent non-linear Y-axis distortion. Slope angles and vertical movements are synchronized to reflect true mathematical rates of change rather than auto-scaled visual suggestions.

PILLAR 03

Mandate-Conditioned Epistemic Governance

When testing Large Language Models on financial data, our protocols enforce explicit institutional decision hurdles (Credit, Trading, Risk, Investment) and require models to halt execution on Decision-Blocking Unknowns.
