03 // COGNITIVE ARCHITECTURE

Visual Filtering & Working Memory

Why unattended visual inputs create measurable upstream cognitive overhead.

The human visual apparatus does not filter sensory inputs at the retinal level. The eye captures the entire field of view and transmits raw spatial data directly to the primary visual cortex (V1/V2).

Conscious attention operates like a narrow spotlight, constrained by the working memory bottleneck ($4 \pm 1$ independent chunks). When instructed to focus on the red circle, the conscious mind isolates the target, while the unconscious brain continues to allocate neural bandwidth to process peripheral shapes.

When the stimulus is removed after 5.0 seconds, participants consistently report recognizing that "something was there", yet fail to recall the exact count or geometric categories.
